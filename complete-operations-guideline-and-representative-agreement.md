## Part 1: Business Model Summary

1.1 This organization will build a centrally controlled ICT product and service network across Bangladesh by appointing one representative in every upazila.

1.2 Upon joining, a representative will make an initial deposit according to the chosen package, which will be split into two parts — a security deposit and a business development fund (details in Part 6). In addition, certain monthly and annual fees (such as a software maintenance fee) will apply.

1.3 A representative will generate business from their area (retail sales, institutional, and government work), and will receive a fixed percentage of the net profit earned as compensation.

## Part 2: Organizational Structure & Accountability

### 2.1 Hierarchy

Head Office (HQ) → Divisional Head → District Head → Upazila Representative.

### 2.2 Dual-Role Structure

2.2.1 The representative of the sadar (headquarters) upazila of each district will simultaneously serve as that district's "District Head" — no separate district office or post will be created.

2.2.2 The head of the sadar district of each division will simultaneously serve as that division's "Divisional Head."

2.2.3 In this way, a qualified representative can earn responsibility and compensation at up to three levels simultaneously (Upazila Representative + District Head + Divisional Head), which will keep administrative costs to a minimum.

### 2.3 Accountability Chain

2.3.1 Every upazila representative will be accountable to their respective District Head.

2.3.2 Every District Head will be accountable to their respective Divisional Head.

2.3.3 All levels (upazila, district, division) will ultimately be accountable to HQ.

2.3.4 Every level will report the work and information collected from its own area to its higher level, while simultaneously keeping HQ directly informed.

## Part 3: Onboarding Process (How It Will Begin)

3.1 **Application:** Interested candidates will apply for a specific upazila.

3.2 **Screening:** HQ will verify the candidate's background, local influence, and business experience.

3.3 **Training:** The selected representative will be trained on product knowledge, system usage, sales process, and rules and regulations.

3.4 **Contract Signing:** A formal contract will be signed between the representative and the company (see Part 8).

3.5 **Package Selection & Deposit:** Based on area size and other relevant considerations, the representative will make an initial deposit according to the chosen package, and in exchange, a receipt for the security deposit and a list of goods/services received under the business development fund will be kept on record signed by both parties (details in Part 6).

3.6 **Start of Operations:** The representative will formally begin operations in their upazila and be connected to the central system.

## Part 4: Day-to-Day Operating Procedure

4.1 **Collecting Work:** Every representative will generate business opportunities from their own upazila — retail/wholesale sales, institutional demand, and open government tenders.

4.2 **Reporting Information:** Collected opportunities, orders, or issues will be reported to the relevant District Head and the central system (HQ).

4.3 **Service Delivery:** HQ will centrally ensure product supply, technical support, training, and financial settlement.

4.4 **Customer Service:** The representative will provide initial resolution of customer complaints and service requests at the local level; complex matters will be forwarded to HQ.

4.5 **Reporting:** Weekly/monthly sales, inventory, and financial reports must be submitted to the central system.
